Safety Key Insights: Total accidents reduced from 141 (FY2009) to 33 (FY2024) — a reduction of 76.6%. Derailments fell from 84 to 20 (76.2% reduction), collisions from 28 to 4 (85.7% reduction). Punctuality Insights: Overall punctuality improved from 74.5% (FY2009) to 87.3% (FY2024). Vande Bharat leads at 93.5%. 📖 Data Dictionary & Provenance
Detailed description and formula documentation for the historical dataset
Variable Name Data Type Formula / Unit Definition & Statistical Context Official Source
passengers.total Integer Millions (Originating) Total passenger bookings starting within the fiscal year. Heavy peak in FY2019 pre-COVID, collapsed in FY2021 by 84.5%. IR Table 1.1
passengers.passengerKm Integer Billion PKM Total distance traveled by all passengers. Obtained by multiplying total passengers originating by the average lead distance. IR Annual Report
revenue.passenger Integer ₹ Crore (₹10M) Gross earnings from passenger transport operations. Correlates highly with passenger-km ($r > 0.9$). Union Budget
revenue.freight Integer ₹ Crore (₹10M) Gross earnings from commodity freight shipping operations. Accounts for ~68% of total revenue. Union Budget
revenue.operatingRatio Float Percentage (%) Operating Ratio = (Working Expenses / Gross Earnings) × 100. Lower is more efficient. Exceeded 170% in FY2021 due to COVID overheads. Audited Accounts
freight.total Integer Million Tonnes (MT) Total tonnage of commodity cargo loaded originating across the network. Highly correlated with GDP growth. Freight Stats
accidents.total Integer Accident Count Sum of train derailments, collisions, fires, and level crossing accidents. Decreased by 76.6% over the 15-year period. Safety Directorate
infrastructure.electrifiedKm Integer Route Kilometers Electrified route length. Electrification grew from 18,274 km (FY2009) to 68,000 km (FY2024), completing 100% broad gauge electrification. Mission Electrification
employees.total Integer Count Total personnel active in operations and management. Has decreased steadily, improving productivity per route kilometer. IR Manpower Table
